Fruit Peeling Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11 (v1.1, ads added)

1. Information we collect

Fruit Peeling (the "App") does not directly collect your name or contact details. The App has no sign-up or login and no separate analytics or tracking tools. However, the App includes Google AdMob advertising, and the information described in section 3 is processed by Google for that purpose.

Weekly leaderboard (optional): only if you tap "post to leaderboard" on the result screen and agree, the following is sent to the leaderboard server:

  • Your nickname (entered by you), score, fruit type and play time
  • A random ID created on your device (to group your records; not a hardware identifier)
  • Your IP address, kept for 1 day only, to limit abuse

Purpose: showing the weekly ranking and preventing fake scores. Retention: weekly records are deleted after 8 weeks, IP records after 1 day. You can delete your records at any time with "Delete my records" in the App.

Nicknames are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname. You can enjoy the whole game without using the leaderboard.

2. Information stored on your device

Scores, the fruit collection, achievements, seeds (the in-game currency), shop purchases, and sound and language settings are stored only on your device (in browser storage) and are never sent elsewhere. They are removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the App.

3. Third parties and advertising

Advertising (Google AdMob): The App shows interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to receive extra in-game seeds) through Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, etc.), ad interaction data and approximate location (inferred from IP address), and this data is sent to Google (including servers outside Korea and your country). The App developer does not directly access this data. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Consent and withdrawal: In regions that require consent (such as the EEA and UK), a Google consent form (UMP) appears when you first open the App; if you decline, personalized ads are limited. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the App under "Settings > Ad privacy settings" (shown in those regions). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device Settings > Google > Ads.

We share no other information with third parties. Leaderboard data is stored by Supabase (database hosting, server location Mumbai, India (ap-south-1)). Leaderboard data is transferred over the internet to this server outside Korea when you post a score. There are no in-app purchases for real money.

4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. The leaderboard is optional and collects only a nickname and score, never contact details.
