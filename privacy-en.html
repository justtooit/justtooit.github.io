Fruit Peeling Privacy Policy

Effective date: REPLACE_DATE (v1.0)

1. Information we collect

Fruit Peeling (the "App") does not collect your name, contact details, location or advertising identifiers. The App has no sign-up or login, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Weekly leaderboard (optional): only if you tap "post to leaderboard" on the result screen and agree, the following is sent to the leaderboard server:

  • Your nickname (entered by you), score, fruit type and play time
  • A random ID created on your device (to group your records; not a hardware identifier)
  • Your IP address, kept for 1 day only, to limit abuse

Purpose: showing the weekly ranking and preventing fake scores. Retention: weekly records are deleted after 8 weeks, IP records after 1 day. You can delete your records at any time with "Delete my records" in the App.

Nicknames are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname. You can enjoy the whole game without using the leaderboard.

2. Information stored on your device

Scores, the fruit collection, achievements, seeds (the in-game currency), shop purchases, and sound and language settings are stored only on your device (in browser storage) and are never sent elsewhere. They are removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the App.

3. Third parties and advertising

We share no information with third parties. Leaderboard data is stored by Supabase (database hosting, server location REPLACE_REGION). The current version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases for real money.

4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. The leaderboard is optional and collects only a nickname and score, never contact details.
