Fruit Peeling Privacy Policy

Effective date: REPLACE_DATE (v1.0)

1. Information we collect

Fruit Peeling (the "App") does not collect or transmit any personal information to any server, including name, contact details, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. The App has no sign-up or login, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

Scores, the fruit collection, achievements, seeds (the in-game currency), shop purchases, and sound and language settings are stored only on your device (in browser storage) and are never sent elsewhere. They are removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the App.

3. Third parties and advertising

We share no information with third parties. The current version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases for real money.

4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
